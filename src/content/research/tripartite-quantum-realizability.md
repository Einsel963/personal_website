title: "Fixed-Measurement Quantum Realizability and Entanglement Certification"
summary: "This project studies which assignments of the eight three-body full correlators in the tripartite (3,2,2) Bell scenario can be realized by a quantum state when the local measurement pairs are fixed. Using measurement-adapted Pauli coordinates and a data-preserving Klein four-group twirl, the work establishes a necessary-and-sufficient realizability criterion and provides explicit realizing states. The same framework characterizes the exact biseparable full-correlation body, yielding a fixed-measurement criterion for genuine multipartite entanglement. These results concern full-correlation data under fixed nondegenerate local projective measurement pairs, rather than complete conditional probability distributions or arbitrary multipartite measurement scenarios."
