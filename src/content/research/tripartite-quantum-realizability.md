This project studies quantum realizability in tripartite Bell scenarios,
especially the three-party, two-setting, two-outcome $(3,2,2)$ scenario. The
central question is: given a set of correlation data and specified measurement
constraints, under what conditions do there exist a quantum state and local
observables that reproduce the data?

The emphasis is not only on the structure of quantum states, but also on how
local measurement settings restrict the realizable correlation domain. This
fixed-observable or constrained-measurement perspective is intended to clarify
how quantum states, observables, and Bell-type correlation data jointly
determine what can be observed.

I am working to extend Lee-Nogami type ideas from bipartite settings to
structured tripartite scenarios. The tools include analytical inequalities,
operator methods, correlation decomposition, semidefinite programming, and the
NPA hierarchy.

The goal is to develop necessary and sufficient conditions in structured
tripartite settings, or at least verifiable and systematically improvable
criteria for quantum realizability. This work is ongoing and is being organized
into a manuscript in preparation.
