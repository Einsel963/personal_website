This is a prospective long-term direction extending the tripartite questions to
general $n$-party Bell scenarios. The aim is to understand quantum
realizability in multipartite systems through unified constraints among quantum
states, local measurements, and correlation data.

The immediate question is which methods from the tripartite setting can scale
meaningfully. Possible ingredients include symmetry reduction,
correlation-tensor decomposition, sector decomposition, operator inequalities,
semidefinite relaxations, and consistency conditions among lower-dimensional
slices of multipartite correlation data.

The long-term goal is to obtain necessary and sufficient conditions, or
systematically convergent hierarchy-based criteria, for multipartite quantum
realizability. At the current stage, this remains a future extension rather than
a completed general theorem or finished numerical implementation.
