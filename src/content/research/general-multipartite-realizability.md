---
title: "Prospective Direction: Quantum Realizability in General Multipartite Systems"
summary: "This prospective project explores whether the fixed-measurement realizability framework can be extended from tripartite correlations to general (n,2,2) multipartite scenarios. The aim is to identify symmetry structures that organize higher-order correlators and to investigate whether group-theoretic reductions, convex optimization, and tensor-network methods can make the resulting realizability problem tractable. This remains an exploratory research direction: no general necessary-and-sufficient criterion, completed theorem, or manuscript is claimed at present."
