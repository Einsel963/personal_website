title: "Tripartite Entanglement Structure and Bell-Nonlocality Boundaries"
summary: "This project examines how different classes of tripartite entanglement are reflected in the mathematical structure of three-body correlation tensors. Using canonical-state representations, polynomial invariants, block decompositions, and singular-value spectra, the work compares fully separable, biseparable, and genuinely multipartite states, including the GHZ and W SLOCC classes. Analytical arguments and high-precision numerical methods are also used to study Bell-violation regions for representative generalized GHZ, W, and mixed-state families, including an exact boundary for a specified class of mixed states. These results concern selected state families and measurement settings rather than a universal Bell-violation boundary for all tripartite mixed states."
