This project investigates fully separable, biseparable, and genuinely
multipartite-entangled sectors in tripartite Bell scenarios. For biseparable
correlations, I derived a CHSH-type factorization and analytically proved the
biseparable Svetlichny bound $|S| \leq 4$.

The work also studies generalized GHZ and W states, together with representative
mixed-state families such as noisy GHZ states, noisy W states, GHZ–W mixtures,
and GHZ–W-noise mixtures. These examples provide controlled settings for
understanding how entanglement structure and local measurement geometry jointly
shape Bell-inequality violations.

Technically, I analyze multipartite correlation tensors through their block
structure, tensor sectors, and singular-value spectra. This helps identify
measurement-sensitive sectors, measurement-induced enhancement, and
branch-switching behavior in parameter-dependent violation regions.

Here, correlation-tensor structure refers to multipartite correlation tensors,
tensor blocks, correlation sectors, singular-value spectra, and tensor
structures of tripartite quantum correlations. It is distinct from
tensor-network methods, which are not part of this project.
