This course report was prepared for coursework related to quantum precision
measurement. It reviews selected ideas in quantum measurement and metrology,
with emphasis on how measurement processes, quantum noise, and physical
resources affect parameter estimation.

The report is included here as a selected coursework report rather than a
formal publication.
